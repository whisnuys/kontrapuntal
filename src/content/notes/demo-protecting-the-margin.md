> Demonstration content — replace before production.

Attention is shaped by what surrounds a task as much as by the task itself. A small margin before beginning can make the difference between choosing the work and merely reacting to the next available prompt.

The useful boundary does not need to be dramatic. It can be one closed tab, one silenced alert, or one deliberate minute in which nothing new is allowed to arrive.
