This placeholder reading note demonstrates how a source can sit beside an interpretation without pretending that the interpretation is a neutral summary.

A useful reading note records friction: where an argument feels strong, where it becomes unclear, and what question remains after the final page. The goal is not to prove that a book was completed. It is to preserve the counterargument it introduced.

That makes the note reusable. Months later, the reader can return not only to a verdict, but to a live question.
