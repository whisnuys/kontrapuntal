The shape of a recorded conversation is partly made after the conversation ends. A pause can remain, disappear, or move. A detour can become the center.

This sample note marks an editorial question worth carrying into future listening: when does a cut clarify an idea, and when does it erase the uncertainty that made the exchange honest?
