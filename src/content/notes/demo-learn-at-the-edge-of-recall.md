> Demonstration content — replace before production.

A useful review sits near the edge of what can be recalled. If every answer arrives instantly, the practice adds little. If nothing can be recovered, the next step is unclear.

The useful middle is modest: enough friction to reveal a gap, and enough familiarity to make the gap workable.
