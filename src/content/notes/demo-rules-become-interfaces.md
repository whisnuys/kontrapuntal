A rule can remain abstract until an interface asks someone to act on it. Games make that translation especially visible: a boundary becomes a choice, a number becomes feedback, and a system becomes something a player can test.

Media performs a similar translation when it turns a technical process into a sequence that can be seen, questioned, and revised.
