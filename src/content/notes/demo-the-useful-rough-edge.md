> Demonstration content — replace before production.

An unfinished draft can reveal its construction. The joins are visible, the weak transitions have not been disguised, and the central question can still move without pulling the whole piece apart.

Polish matters later. Early in the work, a rough edge is useful because it shows where another decision is still possible.
