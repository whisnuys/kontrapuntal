“Understand the subject” is too large to begin. A smaller question creates a surface to work on: one term to define, one assumption to test, one example to explain without notes.

![White, pink, and tan running shoe floating against a pink background.](https://res.cloudinary.com/c8tgycuw/image/upload/v1788536390/samples/shoe.jpg)

Small questions are not small ambitions. They are how an ambition becomes a practice.
