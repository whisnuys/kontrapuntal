> Demonstration content — replace before production.

Public issues arrive already named. The name determines what appears relevant, which comparisons feel natural, and which remedies sound reasonable.

An editorial publication should slow that process down. Before adding another position, it can inspect the frame: who chose the terms, what the terms leave outside, and whether two sides are answering the same question.

This is not an argument for endless qualification. It is an argument for knowing which claim is actually being made before responding to it.
