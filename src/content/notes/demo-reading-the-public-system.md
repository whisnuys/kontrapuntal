> Demonstration content — replace before production.

A technical system contains an argument about how people should move, decide, and share responsibility. Reading that system closely means paying attention to what it permits, what it assumes, and which choices it makes difficult to see.

Books can slow this process down long enough for the hidden argument to become legible.
